# Kenneth A. Adams

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="Kenneth A. Adams — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Contract law](../README.md#specialty-contract-law)

Contract-drafting specialist and author of A Manual of Style for Contract
Drafting, published by the American Bar Association. His work examines how
language, structure and consistent usage affect the clarity and interpretation
of contracts.

## Suggested uses

- Review contract language for ambiguity, unnecessary complexity and
  inconsistent usage.
- Examine whether wording clearly expresses obligations, conditions and
  discretion.
- Compare alternative formulations while preserving the intended allocation of
  rights and responsibilities.

# Richard Feynman

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Richard Feynman — AI-generated portrait" width="240" height="240">

[Physics](../README.md#specialty-physics) · [Quantum electrodynamics](../README.md#specialty-quantum-electrodynamics)

Theoretical physicist and teacher whose contributions to quantum
electrodynamics shared the 1965 Nobel Prize in Physics. His lectures connect
physical intuition with mathematical explanations, while his public writing
examines evidence, uncertainty and scientific integrity.

His historical work informs this perspective; contemporary research questions
require current evidence, and expertise in physics does not establish expertise
in unrelated disciplines.

- Review whether a scientific argument reports contrary evidence and possible sources of self-deception.

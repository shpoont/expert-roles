# David Aaker

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of David Aaker" width="240" height="240">

[Brand management](../README.md#specialty-brand-management) · [Brand equity](../README.md#specialty-brand-equity) · [Marketing management](../README.md#specialty-marketing-management)

Marketing researcher and author known for *Managing Brand Equity* and the
Aaker brand identity model. His public work explains how organizations build,
differentiate and manage brands and brand portfolios.

## Suggested uses

- Clarify a brand's identity and the associations it should build.
- Review how brand choices contribute to customer value and brand equity.
- Assess the roles and relationships of brands within a portfolio.

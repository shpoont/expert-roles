# Don Norman

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Don Norman speaking at dConstruct 2011" width="240" height="240">

[Human-centered design](../README.md#specialty-human-centered-design) · [Cognitive psychology](../README.md#specialty-cognitive-psychology) · [Human-computer interaction](../README.md#specialty-human-computer-interaction)

Design thinker and author whose work explores how people understand, use and
experience products. His books include *The Design of Everyday Things*,
*Emotional Design* and *Design for a Better World*.

## Suggested uses

- Understand why a product feels confusing or difficult to learn.
- Explore the relationship between a system's behavior and people's expectations.
- Discuss how design decisions affect human experience.

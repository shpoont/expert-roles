# Hau Lee

[Supply chain management](../README.md#specialty-supply-chain-management) · [Logistics](../README.md#specialty-logistics) · [Operations management](../README.md#specialty-operations-management)

Operations scholar known for research on supply chains, the bullwhip effect and
the Triple-A supply-chain framework. His work examines how information,
incentives and network design affect coordination, adaptability and performance
across organizations.

## Suggested uses

- Examine how demand information and inventory decisions amplify or reduce
  supply-chain variability.
- Review a supply network for agility, adaptability and alignment between
  participants.
- Assess trade-offs between efficiency, resilience and responsibility in supply-
  chain design.

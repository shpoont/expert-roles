## Suggested uses

- Draft and evaluate concise clinical checklists around critical omissions,
  clear pause points and team communication.
- Examine adoption, workflow fit and measured patient outcomes, rather than
  treating the existence of a checklist as evidence that care improved.
- Distinguish findings from healthcare research from proposed adaptations to
  other kinds of complex professional work.

# Immanuel Kant

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Painted portrait of Immanuel Kant by Johann Gottlieb Becker, 1768" width="240" height="240">

[Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics) · [Epistemology](../README.md#specialty-epistemology)

German philosopher known for critical philosophy and an account of morality
centered on duty, autonomy and the categorical imperative. His work examines
the conditions and limits of knowledge as well as the principles that can
justify action and respect for persons.

An independent AI perspective inspired by public work; it does not represent
Immanuel Kant.

## Suggested uses

- Examine whether a proposed principle can be applied consistently as a
  universal rule.
- Review a decision’s treatment of people, autonomy and moral duties.
- Clarify the assumptions and limits behind a claim to knowledge.

## Explore the work

- [Groundwork, published in this translation as Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682).
- [Kant’s moral philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-moral/).
- [Kant: critical philosophy and the limits of knowledge](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant/).

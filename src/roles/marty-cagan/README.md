Inspired. His product leadership experience and books, including Empowered with
Chris Jones, examine discovery, team autonomy and the responsibilities of
product leaders.

## Suggested uses

- Review whether product discovery addresses customer needs, usability,
  feasibility and business viability.
- Examine how product teams receive problems, make decisions and remain
  accountable for outcomes.
- Challenge product plans that substitute a feature list for a clear problem and
  supporting evidence.

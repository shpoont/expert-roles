# Milan Zelený

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Milan Zelený" width="240" height="240">

[Multiple-criteria decision analysis](../README.md#specialty-multiple-criteria-decision-analysis) · [Operations management](../README.md#specialty-operations-management)

Management scientist known for multiple-criteria decision making and human
systems management. His work examines decisions with competing objectives and
the integration of knowledge, people and production in management systems.

## Suggested uses

- Examine alternatives and trade-offs when several objectives conflict.
- Review how knowledge and decision-making connect to production and operations.
- Question whether coordinating separate functions is enough to improve the whole system.

## Explore the work

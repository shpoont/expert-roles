# David Premack

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="David Premack — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Comparative psychology](../README.md#specialty-comparative-psychology) · [Operant conditioning](../README.md#specialty-operant-conditioning) · [Cognitive psychology](../README.md#specialty-cognitive-psychology)

Experimental psychologist known for the Premack principle of reinforcement
and research on animal cognition. With Guy Woodruff, he introduced the
theory-of-mind question into comparative psychology through their work with
chimpanzees.

## Suggested uses

- Examine a reinforcement plan using the relative preference for actual
  behaviors, rather than assuming a reward has universal value.
- Scrutinize claims about animal understanding, distinguishing observed
  behavior from inferences about beliefs or intentions.
- Compare learning and cognition across species while checking what the
  experimental design can establish.

## Explore the work

- [University of Pennsylvania account of his research and career](https://almanac.upenn.edu/articles/david-premack-psychology).

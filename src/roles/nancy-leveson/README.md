[System safety](../README.md#specialty-system-safety) · [Systems engineering](../README.md#specialty-systems-engineering) · [Software engineering](../README.md#specialty-software-engineering)

MIT researcher in system and software safety, and developer of the STAMP
accident model and STPA analysis approach. Her work examines how control,
feedback and organizational conditions contribute to losses in complex systems.

## Suggested uses

- Map controllers, controlled processes, constraints and feedback to examine
  hazardous interactions beyond individual component failures.
- Identify unsafe control actions and the assumptions behind proposed safety
  constraints, including missing, delayed or misleading feedback.
- Review the organizational context of automation and handoffs, distinguishing
  a structured hazard analysis from evidence that a system is safe in operation.

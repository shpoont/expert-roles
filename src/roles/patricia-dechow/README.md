# Patricia Dechow

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="Patricia Dechow — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Financial accounting](../README.md#specialty-financial-accounting) · [Earnings quality](../README.md#specialty-earnings-quality)

Accounting scholar known for research on earnings quality, accruals and
financial reporting. Her work, including Earnings Quality with Catherine
Schrand, examines what reported profits reveal about performance and how
accounting choices can distort that picture.

## Suggested uses

- Examine whether reported earnings reflect underlying operating performance and
  cash generation.
- Review accounting choices, accruals and incentives that may affect the quality
  of reported results.
- Assess what financial statements can support and what additional evidence is
  needed.

# John Stuart Mill

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Photographic portrait of John Stuart Mill, circa 1870" width="240" height="240">

[Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics) · [Political philosophy](../README.md#specialty-political-philosophy)

British philosopher whose books Utilitarianism and On Liberty develop
influential accounts of human well-being, individual freedom and public
discussion. His work connects the consequences of conduct with questions about
individuality, social authority and the limits of interference.

## Suggested uses

- Examine consequences for well-being alongside the value of individuality.
- Review a proposed restriction on freedom and its justification in preventing
  harm.
- Assess how dissent and open discussion contribute to understanding.

## Explore the work

- [On Liberty](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34901).
- [John Stuart Mill: utilitarianism, liberty and public reasoning](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mill/).

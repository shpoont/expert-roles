# Aswath Damodaran

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Aswath Damodaran" width="240" height="240">

[Corporate finance](../README.md#specialty-corporate-finance) · [Valuation (finance)](../README.md#specialty-valuation-finance)

Finance educator and author known for company valuation and corporate finance.
His public books, teaching materials and spreadsheets connect business
narratives with cash flows, risk and valuation assumptions.

Aswath Damodaran.

## Suggested uses

- Examine the assumptions connecting a business narrative to a valuation.
- Compare valuation methods and identify what drives their differences.
- Review financing and investment choices against cash flows, risk and value.

# Philip Kotler

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Philip Kotler" width="240" height="240">

[Marketing management](../README.md#specialty-marketing-management) · [Strategic management](../README.md#specialty-strategic-management)

Marketing scholar and author known for *Marketing Management*. His public
work connects understanding customer needs with segmentation, positioning
and the design of a coherent marketing strategy.

## Suggested uses

- Clarify the customers, needs and value proposition behind a marketing plan.
- Review segmentation and positioning against a chosen market.
- Connect product, pricing, distribution and communication choices into a strategy.

# Aristotle

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Roman marble bust depicting Aristotle at Palazzo Altemps" width="240" height="240">

[Virtue ethics](../README.md#specialty-virtue-ethics) · [Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics) · [Logic](../README.md#specialty-logic)

Greek philosopher whose surviving work made foundational contributions to
ethics, logic and other fields. The Nicomachean Ethics examines character,
human flourishing and practical wisdom; his logical works study the forms of
reasoning. His historical theories provide philosophical perspectives whose
present-day application requires interpretation.

## Suggested uses

- Examine what good judgment requires in a particular situation.
- Review how habits, character and competing goods shape an ethical decision.
- Analyze the premises and structure of an argument.

[Artificial intelligence](../README.md#specialty-artificial-intelligence) · [Machine learning](../README.md#specialty-machine-learning) · [Deep learning](../README.md#specialty-deep-learning)

AI researcher and educator known for machine-learning teaching and practical
AI adoption. His public work includes technical courses, research and the
*AI Transformation Playbook*.

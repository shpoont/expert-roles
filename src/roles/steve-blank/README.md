# Steve Blank

[Entrepreneurship](../README.md#specialty-entrepreneurship) · [Customer development](../README.md#specialty-customer-development)

Entrepreneur and educator known for developing Customer Development. His books
*The Four Steps to the Epiphany* and *The Startup Owner's Manual* describe how
startups can test assumptions about customers and business models.

Steve Blank.

## Suggested uses

- Identify the customer and business assumptions that need evidence first.
- Design customer interviews and experiments before scaling a startup.
- Distinguish searching for a repeatable business model from executing one.

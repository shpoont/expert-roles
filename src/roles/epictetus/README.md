# Epictetus

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Later engraved depiction of Epictetus by Henri Bonnart" width="240" height="240">

[Stoicism](../README.md#specialty-stoicism) · [Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics)

Stoic philosopher and teacher whose Discourses and Enchiridion were preserved
by his student Arrian. His teaching examines judgment, choice, integrity and
responsibilities to others, with a central distinction between what depends on
us and what lies outside our control.

## Suggested uses

- Examine which judgments and choices are within a person’s agency.
- Review a response to setbacks in light of commitments and responsibilities.
- Challenge assumptions that make well-being depend entirely on external
  outcomes.

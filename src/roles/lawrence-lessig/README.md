# Lawrence Lessig

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Lawrence Lessig" width="240" height="240">

[Copyright legislation](../README.md#specialty-copyright-legislation) · [Information technology law](../README.md#specialty-information-technology-law)

Legal scholar and author known for work on technology, copyright and
regulation. His books examine how law, software architecture, markets and
social norms shape what people can do in a digital environment.

## Suggested uses

- Examine how technical design and legal rules jointly shape user choices.
- Compare copyright and openness trade-offs in a digital product or policy.
- Identify the regulatory assumptions embedded in a technology proposal.

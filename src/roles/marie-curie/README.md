# Marie Curie

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Marie Curie — AI-generated portrait" width="240" height="240">

[Physics](../README.md#specialty-physics) · [Chemistry](../README.md#specialty-chemistry) · [Radiochemistry](../README.md#specialty-radiochemistry)

Physicist and chemist whose research on radioactivity and the discovery
of polonium and radium earned Nobel Prizes in Physics and Chemistry. Her
public scientific work documents measurement, chemical separation and the
evidence used to investigate radioactive substances.

This historical perspective does not supply present-day radiation-safety
procedures or medical expertise; current scientific and practical claims require
contemporary evidence.

## Suggested uses

- Examine how measurements support a physical or chemical explanation, including alternative interpretations.
- Explain the historical development of radioactivity research and the evidence behind the identification of new elements.
- Review the relationship between an experimental question, measurement method and reproducible observations.

# Robert S. Kaplan

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Robert S. Kaplan" width="240" height="240">

[Management accounting](../README.md#specialty-management-accounting) · [Activity-based costing](../README.md#specialty-activity-based-costing) · [Strategic management](../README.md#specialty-strategic-management)

Accounting researcher and co-developer of activity-based costing and the
Balanced Scorecard. His public work connects cost information, performance
measures and the execution of organizational strategy.

## Suggested uses

- Review whether cost allocations explain the resources activities consume.
- Connect performance measures with an organization's strategic objectives.
- Identify gaps between a reporting system and the decisions managers need to make.

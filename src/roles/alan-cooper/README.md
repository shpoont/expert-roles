# Alan Cooper

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Alan Cooper speaking at TNW Conference 2012" width="240" height="240">

[Interaction design](../README.md#specialty-interaction-design) · [Human-computer interaction](../README.md#specialty-human-computer-interaction)

Software designer and author associated with goal-directed design, personas
and scenarios. His books include *About Face* and *The Inmates Are Running the
Asylum*.

## Suggested uses

- Explore how a product should behave to help people achieve their goals.
- Discuss users, scenarios, interaction flows and product behavior.
- Reconsider a feature list through the goals it is meant to serve.

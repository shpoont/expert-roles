Design researcher and co-founder of Mule Design, and author of *Just Enough
Research* and *Conversational Design*. Her work connects clear research
questions, critical thinking and evidence about people with design decisions.

## Suggested uses

- Clarify what a research plan needs to learn and which methods fit the question.
- Examine assumptions, bias and the strength of evidence behind a design decision.
- Assess whether findings address people's needs and the decision a team must make.

Strategy scholar known for research on alliances and business combinations. His
book Remix Strategy examines how organizations combine resources, govern
partnerships and share the value they create together.

## Suggested uses

- Examine what joint value a proposed partnership can create and why cooperation
  is needed.
- Review governance, decision rights and incentives across collaborating
  organizations.
- Challenge assumptions about how partners share value, risks and investments.

# Geoffrey Moore

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Geoffrey Moore" width="240" height="240">

[Marketing management](../README.md#specialty-marketing-management) · [Technology adoption lifecycle](../README.md#specialty-technology-adoption-lifecycle) · [Strategic management](../README.md#specialty-strategic-management)

Business author known for *Crossing the Chasm*. His work examines how
technology businesses move from early adopters to mainstream customers
through focused markets, positioning and a complete customer solution.

## Suggested uses

- Choose a focused initial market for a technology product.
- Identify gaps between an early-adopter offering and mainstream customer needs.
- Review positioning, partners and adoption barriers in a market-entry plan.

# Amy Edmondson

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Amy Edmondson" width="240" height="240">

[Psychological safety](../README.md#specialty-psychological-safety) · [Organizational learning](../README.md#specialty-organizational-learning)

Management scholar known for research on psychological safety, teaming and
learning in organizations. Her research and books, including The Fearless
Organization, examine how people speak up, collaborate and learn from mistakes.

An independent AI perspective inspired by public work; it does not represent
Amy Edmondson.

## Suggested uses

- Examine whether team practices make it possible to raise concerns, ask
  questions and admit mistakes.
- Review how a team learns from failures while maintaining accountability and
  performance expectations.
- Analyze collaboration and learning across professional or organizational
  boundaries.

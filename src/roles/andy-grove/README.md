Business leader and author of *High Output Management* and *Only the Paranoid
Survive*. His public work examines managerial effectiveness, organizational
output and how companies respond to strategic inflection points.

## Suggested uses

- Identify where a manager's decisions and activities most affect team output.
- Review operating practices, meetings and measures as an organization grows.
- Examine whether a change in the business environment requires a strategic response.

# Karl Popper

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Karl Popper — AI-generated portrait" width="240" height="240">

[Philosophy of science](../README.md#specialty-philosophy-of-science) · [Epistemology](../README.md#specialty-epistemology)

Philosopher of science known for critical rationalism, the problem of
demarcating science from non-science and the growth of knowledge through
criticism. His work distinguishes testability from conclusive proof and treats
the acceptance of an apparent refutation as a methodological judgment.

## Suggested uses

- Examine what observations could conflict with an explanatory claim.
- Review whether a test exposes a theory to meaningful criticism.
- Assess the assumptions and possible errors behind an apparent refutation.

# Jordan Peterson

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Jordan Peterson" width="240" height="240">

[Personality psychology](../README.md#specialty-personality-psychology)

Psychologist and author known for research on personality and the Big Five,
and for *Maps of Meaning* and *12 Rules for Life*. His public work connects
personality differences with meaning, personal responsibility and pursuing goals.

An independent AI perspective inspired by public work; it does not represent
Jordan Peterson.

## Suggested uses

- Examine how personality differences affect motivation, work habits and
  interpersonal friction.
- Review goals and commitments through personal responsibility and meaningful
  action.
- Analyze a narrative or argument about order, uncertainty and meaning.

## Explore the work

- [Between facets and domains: 10 aspects of the Big Five](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17983306/),
  coauthored with Colin G. DeYoung and Lena C. Quilty (2007).
- [Public work, books and teaching](https://www.jordanbpeterson.com/about/),
  including *Maps of Meaning* and *12 Rules for Life*.

# Ed Frawley

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="Ed Frawley — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Dog training](../README.md#specialty-dog-training)

Professional dog trainer, founder of Leerburg and producer of dog-training
instruction since 1982. His courses and articles cover obedience, marker
training, reward delivery and everyday management of dogs in the home.

Ed Frawley.

## Suggested uses

- Review an obedience plan for clear steps, consistent cues and manageable
  distractions.
- Examine marker timing, reward delivery and communication between dog and
  handler.
- Review household routines and management choices that affect a dog's
  training.

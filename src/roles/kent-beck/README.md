# Kent Beck

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Kent Beck" width="240" height="240">

[Software engineering](../README.md#specialty-software-engineering) · [Test-driven development](../README.md#specialty-test-driven-development) · [Extreme programming](../README.md#specialty-extreme-programming)

Software developer and author known for Extreme Programming and test-driven
development. His books and essays examine feedback, small design changes and
sustainable ways for teams to develop software.

## Suggested uses

- Break a risky software change into small steps with fast feedback.
- Choose tests that guide design and reveal meaningful behavior changes.
- Review engineering practices around collaboration, feedback and delivery.

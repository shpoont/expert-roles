# Jeanne Ross

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Jeanne Ross — AI-generated portrait" width="240" height="240">

[Enterprise architecture](../README.md#specialty-enterprise-architecture)

Information-systems researcher known for work on enterprise architecture,
operating models and digital business design at MIT CISR. Her coauthored books
examine how organizations connect processes, data, technology and accountability
to execute strategy.

## Suggested uses

- Review whether shared processes, data and technology fit the intended
  operating model.
- Examine the organizational capabilities and accountability needed for a
  digital initiative.
- Distinguish installing technology from changing how an organization delivers
  value.

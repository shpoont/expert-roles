Software author known for *Refactoring* and *Patterns of Enterprise Application
Architecture*. His public work connects software design, incremental improvement
and the practical trade-offs of building business systems.

Martin Fowler.

## Suggested uses

- Review a software design and explain its most consequential trade-offs.
- Plan small refactorings that improve structure while preserving behavior.
- Compare architectural patterns against the needs of a business system.

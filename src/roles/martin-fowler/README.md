# Martin Fowler

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Martin Fowler" width="240" height="240">

[Software architecture](../README.md#specialty-software-architecture) · [Software engineering](../README.md#specialty-software-engineering) · [Software refactoring](../README.md#specialty-software-refactoring)

Software author known for *Refactoring* and *Patterns of Enterprise Application
Architecture*. His public work connects software design, incremental improvement
and the practical trade-offs of building business systems.

[Codex role file](../../../dist/codex/expert-martin-fowler.toml) ·

An independent AI perspective inspired by public work; it does not represent
Martin Fowler.

## Suggested uses

- Review a software design and explain its most consequential trade-offs.
- Plan small refactorings that improve structure while preserving behavior.
- Compare architectural patterns against the needs of a business system.

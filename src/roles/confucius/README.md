# Confucius

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Yuan-dynasty painted depiction of Confucius by an anonymous artist" width="240" height="240">

[Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics) · [Political philosophy](../README.md#specialty-political-philosophy)

Chinese philosopher and teacher associated with moral cultivation, learning,
ritual and responsible government. The Analects preserves sayings and
conversations attributed to him and his disciples. This profile emphasizes that
early textual tradition; its compilation and interpretation should be
distinguished from later doctrines associated with his name.

## Suggested uses

- Examine responsibilities and reciprocity within a relationship or social
  role.
- Review leadership through personal example, trust and moral conduct.
- Consider how habits, learning and shared practices shape character.

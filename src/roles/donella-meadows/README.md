Environmental scientist and systems thinker, lead author of The Limits to Growth
and a 1994 MacArthur Fellow. Her work explains how feedback, delays and system
structure shape behavior over time.

## Suggested uses

- Trace patterns over time through stocks, flows, reinforcing or balancing
  feedback and delays, rather than attributing every outcome to an isolated event.
- Compare interventions in parameters, information, rules and goals, making
  assumptions about their effects explicit.
- Examine system boundaries and unintended consequences; treat leverage-point
  rankings as prompts for inquiry rather than universal guarantees.

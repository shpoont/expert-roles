# Mark Roberge

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="Mark Roberge — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Sales management](../README.md#specialty-sales-management)

Sales practitioner and author of The Sales Acceleration Formula, drawing on his
work building the sales organization at HubSpot. His approach examines how
hiring, training, coaching and measurement shape a repeatable sales system.

## Suggested uses

- Review a sales hiring and onboarding process for consistent criteria and
  useful feedback.
- Examine how performance measures inform individual coaching and sales-
  management decisions.
- Assess the assumptions behind scaling a sales team and aligning sales with
  demand generation.

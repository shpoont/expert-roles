# Socrates

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Roman marble depiction of Socrates at the Louvre" width="240" height="240">

[Socratic method](../README.md#specialty-socratic-method) · [Ethics](../README.md#specialty-ethics)

Athenian philosopher known for examining knowledge, virtue and how to live
through dialogue. He left no writings; his thought survives in accounts by
others, especially Plato and Xenophon. This profile draws principally on the
Socratic questioning portrayed in Plato’s early dialogues; reconstructing the
historical Socrates from those accounts remains uncertain.

Socrates.

## Suggested uses

- Clarify contested definitions and the assumptions behind an argument.
- Examine whether beliefs and ethical judgments remain consistent under
  counterexamples.
- Distinguish an acknowledged limit of knowledge from an unsupported claim.

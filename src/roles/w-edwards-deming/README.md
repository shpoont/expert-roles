# W. Edwards Deming

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of W. Edwards Deming" width="240" height="240">

[Quality management](../README.md#specialty-quality-management) · [Statistical process control](../README.md#specialty-statistical-process-control) · [Operations management](../README.md#specialty-operations-management)

Statistician and management thinker known for advancing quality improvement
and understanding variation in systems. His books, Fourteen Points and System
of Profound Knowledge connect process performance with management practice.

## Suggested uses

- Distinguish routine process variation from evidence of a meaningful change.
- Review incentives and management choices that shape system performance.
- Plan learning cycles that improve quality across an entire process.

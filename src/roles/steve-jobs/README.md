# Steve Jobs

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Steve Jobs" width="240" height="240">

[Product design](../README.md#specialty-product-design) · [Strategic management](../README.md#specialty-strategic-management) · [Entrepreneurship](../README.md#specialty-entrepreneurship)

Apple co-founder and product leader whose public talks and interviews explore
product focus, simplicity and the integration of hardware, software and customer
experience. Apple's documented design practice also shows the collaboration
behind the products he helped bring to market.

## Suggested uses

- Challenge a product portfolio's focus and identify choices that dilute it.
- Review whether hardware, software and the customer experience form a coherent product.
- Examine how product priorities and simplicity shape business strategy.

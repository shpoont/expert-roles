# Pieter Levels

<img src="placeholder.svg" alt="Pieter Levels — name placeholder" width="240" height="240">

[Entrepreneurship](../README.md#specialty-entrepreneurship)

Entrepreneur and independent product builder known for Nomad List (now
Nomads.com), Remote OK and Photo AI. His book *MAKE* and public writing describe
his approach to building internet businesses without outside funding.

An independent AI perspective inspired by public work; it does not represent
Pieter Levels.

## Suggested uses

- Examine an internet-business idea through a solo founder's constraints,
  customer needs and willingness to pay.
- Review a launch plan for unnecessary scope, delayed charging and avoidable
  operating costs.
- Evaluate automation and simple technical choices for a small, bootstrapped
  business.

Entrepreneur and author of *The Mom Test*, a practical guide to learning from
customer conversations. His work emphasizes concrete behavior, questions that
reduce biased feedback, and commitments that help assess early customer interest.

## Suggested uses

- Review discovery questions for leading wording, hypothetical answers and pitching.
- Distinguish evidence about an existing problem from compliments about an idea.
- Examine whether proposed commitments meaningfully test early customer interest.

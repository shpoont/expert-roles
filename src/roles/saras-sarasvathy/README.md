# Saras Sarasvathy

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Portrait of Saras Sarasvathy" width="240" height="240">

[Entrepreneurship](../README.md#specialty-entrepreneurship) · [Effectuation](../README.md#specialty-effectuation)

Entrepreneurship researcher known for developing effectuation, an approach to
creating ventures under uncertainty. Her research examines how experienced
entrepreneurs start with available means, limit affordable loss and build
commitments with others.

## Suggested uses

- Choose useful next steps when goals and market conditions are uncertain.
- Design small commitments around available skills, relationships and resources.
- Evaluate venture experiments through affordable loss and stakeholder commitment.

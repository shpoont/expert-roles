# Jeff Hawkins

<img src="portrait.jpg" alt="Jeff Hawkins — AI-generated portrait" width="240" height="240">

[Artificial intelligence](../README.md#specialty-artificial-intelligence) · [Computational neuroscience](../README.md#specialty-computational-neuroscience)

Scientist and inventor, cofounder of Numenta and author of *On Intelligence*
and *A Thousand Brains*. His research explores theories of cortical learning
and how those ideas could inform sensorimotor AI.

## Suggested uses

- Examine an AI design through sensorimotor learning, reference frames and
  models of the world.
- Compare assumptions about intelligence with the Thousand Brains theory.
- Scrutinize how a proposed brain-inspired system connects its neuroscience
  hypotheses to testable computational behavior.

## Explore the work

- [Thousand Brains Project biography and books](https://thousandbrains.org/about/team/),
  including *On Intelligence* (with Sandra Blakeslee) and *A Thousand Brains*.
- [The Thousand Brains Project: A New Paradigm for Sensorimotor Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18354),
  coauthored with Viviane Clay and Niels Leadholm (2024).

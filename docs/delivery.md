# Delivery and handover

## Use and handover

The current result is a public documentation scaffold. Start at
[README.md](../README.md), then the [agreement](../PROJECT.md),
[current work](current-work.md) and [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
There are no roles to install or run and no consumer runtime prerequisites.

Leon.id Komarovsky retains direction and acceptance responsibility. Public
[issues](https://github.com/shpoont/expert-roles/issues) are the contribution and
support route for this library. No responsibility for an independent integration
is transferred by this scaffold.

## Delivery acceptance and remaining work

For the current structure-alignment boundary, inspect the connected records,
retained catalogue and decisions, public suitability and link checks. See
[checks](../tests/README.md) and [evidence](../evidence/README.md) for status and
limits. A committed or pushed scaffold does not establish owner acceptance or
role effectiveness.

The overall project remains open for role design, format, license, contribution
terms and evaluation. This task creates no release or closure decision.

## Outcomes and learning

No role-use or contributor outcome assessment has run. Before those assessments
are handed over, agree their owner, timing or trigger, method and required
evidence, and obtain the receiving owner's acceptance of that responsibility.
Record useful findings in [current work](current-work.md) and the relevant
design or decision records.
